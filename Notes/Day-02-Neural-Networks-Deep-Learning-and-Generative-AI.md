## 1. Neural Networks

**Simple definition:**  
Neural networks are models inspired by the **human brain**, made of layers of connected neurons.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- Neural networks help the chatbot understand whether the user wants:
    
    - Refund
        
    - Delivery status
        
    - Cancellation
        

**Explanation:**  
They help AI **recognize patterns** in user messages.

---

## 2. Deep Learning

**Simple definition:**  
Deep learning uses **large neural networks with many layers** to understand complex data.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- The chatbot understands:
    
    - “Where is my order?”
        
    - “My package hasn’t arrived”
        

Both mean the same thing.

**Explanation:**  
Deep learning helps AI handle **different ways of asking the same question**.

---

## 3. Generative AI (Gen AI)

**Simple definition:**  
Generative AI **creates new content**, instead of choosing from fixed answers.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- The chatbot generates a **new reply** for every user query.
    

**Explanation:**  
Responses are not copied.  
They are **created dynamically**.

---

## 4. Tokens

**Simple definition:**  
Tokens are **small pieces of text** that AI models understand.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- “Where is my order?” becomes:
    
    - Where
        
    - is
        
    - my
        
    - order
        

**Explanation:**  
AI does not read sentences like humans.  
It reads **tokens**.

---

## 5. Forward Propagation

**Simple definition:**  
Data moves **from input to output** to make a prediction.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- User message → model → chatbot reply
    

**Explanation:**  
This step is used to **generate an answer**.

---

## 6. Backpropagation

**Simple definition:**  
The model **learns from mistakes** by correcting errors.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- If a chatbot gives a wrong reply, the model updates itself during training.
    

**Explanation:**  
This is how AI **improves accuracy over time**.

---

## 7. Transformers

**Simple definition:**  
Transformers are models that **read all words at once** and understand context.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- The chatbot understands long conversations and follow-up questions.
    

**Explanation:**  
Transformers allow AI to understand:

- Meaning
    
- Context
    
- Relationships between words
    

---

## 8. Parallelism

**Simple definition:**  
Parallelism means processing **many tokens at the same time**.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- The chatbot responds instantly, even to long messages.
    

**Explanation:**  
Without parallelism, chatbots would be slow.
