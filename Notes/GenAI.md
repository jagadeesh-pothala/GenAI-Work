**1. Intelligence

Definition: The ability to learn, understand, reason, and apply knowledge to solve problems and adapt to new situations.

Common example (chatbot):

- The chatbot understands customer questions and provides correct answers instead of fixed replies.
    

---

## 2. Human Intelligence

Definition: Natural intelligence of humans that includes logic, emotions, creativity, judgment, and social understanding.

Common example (chatbot):

- A human support agent understands customer frustration and responds politely; this behavior is what AI tries to imitate.
    

---

## 3. Structured and Unstructured Data

### Structured Data

Definition: Data organized in a fixed format (rows and columns).

Common example (chatbot):

- Order details stored in a database (order ID, product name, price).
    

### Unstructured Data

Definition: Data without a predefined structure.

Common example (chatbot):

- Customer messages like "My order hasn’t arrived yet".
    

---

## 4. Machine Learning (ML)

Definition: A technique where systems learn patterns from data and improve automatically without explicit rules.

Common example (chatbot):

- The chatbot learns which responses work best based on past customer interactions.
    

**

## 5. Neural Networks

Definition: Brain-inspired models made of layers of connected neurons that learn patterns from data.

Common example (chatbot):

- Neural networks help the chatbot recognize user intent such as refund, delivery, or cancellation.
    

---

## 6. Deep Learning

Definition: A subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with many hidden layers to learn complex patterns.

Common example (chatbot):

- Deep learning allows the chatbot to understand different ways of asking the same question.
    

---

## 7. Generative AI (Gen AI)

Definition: AI systems that generate new content such as text, images, audio, video, or code.

**Common example (chatbot):

- The chatbot generates a new, natural-language response for every customer query.
    

---

## 8. Tokens

Definition: Small units of text (words or word parts) that AI models process.

Common example (chatbot):

- The sentence "Where is my order?" is broken into tokens so the chatbot can understand it.

**

## 9. Propagation

### Forward Propagation

Definition: Data flows from input to output to make predictions.

Common example (chatbot):

- User message flows through the model to predict the correct response.
    

### 10.Backpropagation

Definition: Errors flow backward to update weights and improve accuracy.

Common example (chatbot):

- The chatbot improves after learning that a response was incorrect.
    

---

## 11. Transformers

Definition: A deep learning architecture that processes all tokens at once using self-attention.

Common example (chatbot):

- Transformers help the chatbot understand full sentences and conversation context.
    

---

## 12. Parallelism

Definition: Processing multiple tokens simultaneously instead of sequentially.

Common example (chatbot):

- The chatbot processes long customer messages instantly instead of word by word.

## 13. Embeddings

Definition: Embeddings are numerical representations (vectors) of data such as text, images, audio, or video that capture meaning or GPS Coordinates of a Word

Why embeddings are needed:

- Computers understand only numbers
    
- Embeddings convert meaning into numbers
