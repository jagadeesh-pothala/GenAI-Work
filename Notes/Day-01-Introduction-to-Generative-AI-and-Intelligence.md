## 1. Intelligence

**definition:**  
Intelligence means the ability to **learn from experience**, **understand situations**, **think logically**, and **use knowledge to solve problems**.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- Instead of giving the same fixed reply, the chatbot **understands the question** and gives a **correct and useful answer**.
    

**Explanation:**  
A normal program follows rules.  
An intelligent system **thinks and decides** based on the situation.

---

## 2. Human Intelligence

**definition:**  
Human intelligence includes **thinking, emotions, creativity, judgment, and social understanding**.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- A human support agent understands when a customer is angry or frustrated and replies politely.
    
- Chatbots try to **copy this behavior**.
    

**Explanation:**  
Humans don’t just answer questions.  
They **understand feelings and context**, which AI tries to imitate.

---

## 3. Structured and Unstructured Data

### Structured Data

**definition:**  
Data that is **well-organized** and stored in tables (rows and columns).

**Common example (chatbot):**

- Order details like:
    
    - Order ID
        
    - Product name
        
    - Price
        

**Explanation:**  
This data is easy for computers to read and search.

---

### Unstructured Data

**definition:**  
Data that has **no fixed format**.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- Customer messages like:  
    _“My order hasn’t arrived yet”_
    

**Explanation:**  
Most chatbot data is unstructured.  
AI is needed to understand it.

---

## 4. Machine Learning (ML)

**definition:**  
Machine Learning allows systems to **learn from data** instead of following fixed rules.

**Common example (chatbot):**

- The chatbot learns which answers satisfy users based on past conversations.
    

**Explanation:**  
The more data the chatbot sees, the **better it becomes**.
